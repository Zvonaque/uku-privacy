# UKU Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 30 August 2026 · Applies to the UKU app ("Učiti kako učiti") during its closed beta._

UKU is a learning-habit app for school-age children and their parents, built by
Zvonimir Jukić. It helps a child plan school activities, check in on them
daily, and share that progress with a linked parent. This policy explains, in
plain language, what data the app handles and what we do with it. It is
deliberately short — the app collects very little.

## What we collect

**Account data.** An email address and a chosen role (child or parent). The
email is used to sign in and to send account emails (such as sign-up
confirmation). We do not ask for names, birth dates, photos, or phone numbers.

**Content you create in the app.** Calendars, activity groups, activities,
scheduled slots, daily check-ins and their review status, events (such as
homework or tests), and short notes between a child and their linked parent.
This content is visible only to the child who created it and the parent(s) the
child's family has explicitly linked through an invitation — never to other
users or the public.

**Notification data.** If notifications are enabled, the app stores a device
push token (an identifier issued by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging that lets
us deliver notifications to that device) and a record of which notifications
were sent.

**Crash data.** If the app crashes, a technical report (the error, a stack
trace, device model, operating system version, app version, and a random
installation identifier) is sent to Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can
find and fix the problem. Crash reports contain no account data and no content
you created — we have configured them to carry no user identifiers.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising, no ad networks, no personalised ads — the app contains no ads at all.
- No behavioural analytics or tracking.
- No location data, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
- No selling or renting of data to anyone, ever.
- No profiling of children.

## Where the data lives

App data is stored with **Supabase** on servers in the **European Union**.
Push-notification delivery and crash reporting are provided by **Google
Firebase** (Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics). These providers process data
only on our behalf to run the service. All data is encrypted in transit.

## Why we process it (legal basis)

We process account data and app content to provide the service a family signs
up for; notification data to deliver the reminders a family has configured
(parent notifications are opt-in); and crash data on our legitimate interest
in keeping the app working. We use the data for nothing else.

## Children's data and parental consent

UKU is designed for children and treats their data accordingly. Under Croatian
law, a child under 16 cannot consent to data processing themselves — **account
creation for a child requires the consent of a parent or legal guardian**.
During the closed beta, accounts are created within a known group of
collaborating families and teachers, with parents involved in setup. If you
believe a child is using UKU without parental consent, contact us and we will
remove the account.

## How long we keep it

Data is kept while the account is active.

## Delete your account

**In the app.** While signed in, open **Settings** (the cog at the top of the
home screen) → **Account** → **Delete Account**, and type the confirmation
phrase shown. This deletes your account and everything it holds — your
calendar, activity groups, activities, check-ins, events, notes, and any link
to a parent or child — straight away. It cannot be undone, and there is no
recovery period.

**By email.** You can also ask us at any time to **delete an account and all
its data**, or to **export a copy of it**, by emailing
**zvonimir.jukic.dev@gmail.com** from the account's email address (or, for a
child's account, from the consenting parent's address). Tell us the email
address of the account to delete. We complete deletion requests within 30 days
and confirm by reply. A parent asking us to delete a child's account uses this
channel: in-app deletion removes only the account that is signed in.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access, correction, export, or deletion of your
(or your child's) data, and object to or restrict processing — all via
zvonimir.jukic.dev@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory
authority; in Croatia that is AZOP (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka,
azop.hr).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will always be at this address,
with the effective date above. During the beta we will also tell testers
directly about any change that matters.
